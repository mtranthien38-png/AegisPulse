# Product Brief

## Mission

Help teams detect infrastructure anomalies quickly and route them into a reliable incident response workflow.

## Primary users

- Validator operators
- Protocol SRE teams
- Security analysts
- Incident commanders

## Key workflows

- Asset onboarding
- Health monitoring
- Alert ingestion
- Incident triage
- Risk scoring
- Resolution tracking

## Non-goals

- Insurance management
- Slashing adjudication
- Governance voting
